# Information you need before you apply

Have the following ready before you start a UI claim:

## Personal information
- Full legal name and any other names used (such as a maiden name)
- Social Security number
- Date of birth
- Mailing address and residential address, phone number, and email address
- California driver license or state ID number, if you have one
- Alien Registration Number (A-number) and work authorization expiration date, if you are not a U.S. citizen
- Your preferred language for EDD communication

## Employment information (last 18 months)
For your most recent employer, and every employer in the last 18 months:
- Employer name exactly as it appears on your pay stub or W-2
- Employer mailing address and phone number
- Start and end dates of employment
- Your gross earnings (before deductions)
- Reason you are no longer working there
- Your job title, and whether you were a union member (union hall information if so)

## Other information
- Whether you are receiving, or will receive, severance pay, vacation pay, a pension, or wages in lieu of notice
- If you worked for the federal government or were in the military: Form SF-8 or DD-214 (Member 4 copy)

Tip: Gather your last pay stubs and W-2s. They show the employer's legal name, address, and your earnings.
