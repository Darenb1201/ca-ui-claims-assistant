# Certifying to get paid

After you file, you must certify for benefits every two weeks to receive payments. Certifying means answering questions to show you remained eligible during each week.

## How to certify
- UI Online (fastest): log in through myEDD and select Certify for Benefits.
- EDD Tele-Cert by phone: use the phone number and instructions on your Notice of Unemployment Insurance Award.
- By mail: complete and return the Continued Claim Form (DE 4581) EDD mails to you.

## Questions you will answer each period
For each week you will be asked whether you:
- Were too sick or injured to work.
- Were available for work every day.
- Looked for work (you may need to list your work search activities).
- Refused any work.
- Started school or training.
- Did any work or earned any money, including part-time, gig, or self-employment income. Report gross earnings for the week you worked, not the week you were paid.
- Received any other income such as a pension or holiday pay.

## Important rules
- Certify on time. If you wait too long, payments can be delayed or denied.
- Report all earnings, even small amounts. Working part-time may reduce, not eliminate, your weekly payment.
- Keep a record of your work search: employer name, date contacted, position, and how you applied.
- If you go back to full-time work, stop certifying and report your return to work.

## Work search requirement
You must make a reasonable effort to look for work each week, unless EDD has told you that you are exempt (for example, if you are on a temporary layoff with a definite return date, or are a member of a union that dispatches through a hiring hall). Free help is available at America's Job Center of California (AJCC) locations and CalJOBS (caljobs.ca.gov), where many claimants must register.
