# How to file an Unemployment Insurance claim in California

## When to file
File as soon as you lose your job or your hours are reduced. Your claim starts the Sunday of the week you apply, so waiting can cost you benefits. You cannot be paid for weeks before you filed unless EDD approves backdating for a good reason.

## Ways to file
1. Online (fastest): Create a myEDD account at myedd.edd.ca.gov and use UI Online. You will verify your identity through ID.me.
2. By phone: Call the EDD UI line at 1-800-300-5616 (Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 12 noon, except state holidays). Spanish and other languages are available; see the EDD contact page for language-specific numbers.
3. By mail or fax: Use the paper Unemployment Insurance Application (DE 1101I), available on the EDD website.

## Step-by-step: filing online
1. Go to myedd.edd.ca.gov and create a myEDD account (or log in).
2. Verify your identity with ID.me when prompted.
3. Select Unemployment Insurance, then open UI Online and choose to file a new claim.
4. Answer the questions about your personal information, your work history for the last 18 months, and why you are no longer working.
5. Review everything carefully and submit. Save or print your confirmation number.
6. Watch your UI Online inbox and your mail for EDD notices and any phone interview appointments.

## Tips to avoid delays
- Use your full legal name exactly as it appears on your Social Security card.
- List every employer from the last 18 months, including part-time work and work in other states.
- Use your employer's exact business name and address as shown on your pay stubs or W-2.
- Answer every question honestly. Mistakes or false statements can delay payment or lead to overpayments and penalties.
- Respond to every EDD request by the deadline listed.
