# Related programs and help

## Other EDD programs
- State Disability Insurance (SDI): for workers who cannot work because of a non-work-related illness, injury, or pregnancy. You cannot receive UI and SDI for the same week.
- Paid Family Leave (PFL): for workers who need time off to care for a seriously ill family member, bond with a new child, or for a military family need.
- Workers' Compensation is for work-related injuries and is handled by the employer's insurer, not EDD.

## Job search and training
- CalJOBS (caljobs.ca.gov): California's online job search system; many UI claimants are required to register.
- America's Job Center of California (AJCC): free in-person help with resumes, interviews, and job training.
- Training Benefits (TB) program: may let you keep receiving UI while in approved training.

## Contact EDD
- UI phone line: 1-800-300-5616 (Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 12 noon).
- TTY: 1-800-815-9387.
- Online: Ask EDD at askedd.edd.ca.gov.
- Phone numbers for Spanish, Chinese, Vietnamese and other languages are listed on the EDD contact page.

## Other assistance while unemployed
- CalFresh (food assistance): getcalfresh.org or your county social services office.
- Medi-Cal and Covered California for health coverage.
- 211 (call or text) for local help with housing, food, and utilities.
