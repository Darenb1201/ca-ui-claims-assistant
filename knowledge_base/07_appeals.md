# Appealing a decision

If EDD denies your claim or finds you ineligible for some weeks, you have the right to appeal.

## Deadline
- You must file your appeal within 30 calendar days from the mailing date of the decision (Notice of Determination, DE 1080CZ, or Notice of Overpayment).
- If you are late, explain your good cause for filing late in the appeal.

## How to file an appeal
- Online through UI Online (myEDD), or
- Complete the Appeal Form (DE 1000M) included with your decision and mail it to the address listed, or
- Write a letter that includes your name, Social Security number (last four digits are typically enough on correspondence), the decision you are appealing, and the reasons you disagree.

## What happens next
- Your case goes to the California Unemployment Insurance Appeals Board (CUIAB), which is separate from EDD.
- An Administrative Law Judge holds a hearing (often by phone or video). You can bring witnesses and documents.
- Keep certifying for benefits every two weeks while your appeal is pending. If you win, you can only be paid for weeks you certified.

## Getting help
Free legal help may be available from legal aid organizations in your county. The CUIAB website (cuiab.ca.gov) has hearing guides.
