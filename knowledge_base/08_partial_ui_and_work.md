# Reduced hours and part-time work

## Partial unemployment
If your employer cut your hours, you may be eligible for partial UI benefits. File a claim as you normally would and report your reduced earnings when you certify.

## How earnings affect your payment
- Working part-time reduces your weekly payment but usually does not eliminate it. EDD does not deduct the first $25 or 25 percent of your weekly gross earnings, whichever is more; the rest is subtracted from your weekly benefit amount.
- Check your certification results and EDD guidance for the current formula.
- Always report gross earnings for the week you performed the work.

## Work Sharing program
Employers facing a slowdown can apply to the EDD Work Sharing Program to reduce hours instead of laying off workers. Affected employees receive a portion of UI benefits.

## Going back to work
- If you return to full-time work, stop certifying.
- If you lose that job later, reopen your existing claim in UI Online instead of filing a new one if your benefit year is still active.
