# ID verification and avoiding fraud

## Identity verification
- EDD uses ID.me to verify identity for myEDD accounts. You will need a photo ID (driver license, state ID, or passport) and a smartphone or computer with a camera. If you cannot verify online, ID.me offers video chat or in-person options, and EDD may mail identity verification requests.

## Protect yourself from scams
- EDD will never ask for your password or full Social Security number by text message or social media.
- Only use official sites ending in .ca.gov (edd.ca.gov, myedd.edd.ca.gov) or id.me.
- Do not pay anyone to file a claim for you. Filing is free.
- Report suspected identity theft or fraud to EDD at edd.ca.gov/fraud.

## Penalties for false statements
Knowingly giving false information to get benefits is fraud. It can result in an overpayment you must pay back, a 30 percent penalty, disqualification from future benefits, and criminal prosecution.

## Overpayments
If EDD determines you were paid benefits you were not eligible for, you will receive a Notice of Overpayment. You can appeal it within 30 days, or request a waiver if the overpayment was not your fault and repayment would cause hardship.
