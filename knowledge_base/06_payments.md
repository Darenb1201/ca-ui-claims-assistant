# Payments and claim status

## How payments are sent
- EDD typically pays UI benefits on an EDD Debit Card issued by Bank of America. You can transfer funds to your own bank account.
- Check UI Online for current payment options available to you.
- Payments usually go out within a few business days after you certify, if there are no eligibility issues.

## Checking the status of a claim or payment
- Log in to UI Online through myEDD to see your claim status, payment history, and messages.
- You can also use the EDD automated phone line on your award notice.

## Common reasons for delays
- Identity verification not completed (ID.me).
- An eligibility interview is pending (for example, about why you left your last job).
- You did not certify, or answered "yes" to a question that requires review.
- EDD is waiting for information from you or your employer.

## Phone interviews
If EDD has a question about your eligibility, they will schedule a phone interview and mail you a Notice of Interview (DE 4800). Be available at the scheduled time, have your documents ready, and answer truthfully. If you miss it, contact EDD right away.
