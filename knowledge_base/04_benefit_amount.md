# Benefit amount and duration

## Weekly benefit amount (WBA)
- California UI weekly benefits range from $40 to $450 per week.
- Your weekly benefit amount is based on the wages you earned in the highest-paid quarter of your base period. Generally it is about 50 percent of your average weekly wages in that quarter, up to the $450 maximum.
- EDD has an online UI Benefit Calculator to estimate your WBA. The official amount is on the Notice of Unemployment Insurance Award (DE 429Z) mailed after you file.

## How long benefits last
- A regular UI claim lasts for a 52-week "benefit year."
- You can receive up to 26 times your weekly benefit amount within that year (generally up to 26 weeks of full benefits). If you receive partial payments, benefits can stretch over more weeks.
- Federal extensions are only available during periods of high unemployment when authorized.

## The base period
The base period is the 12-month period EDD uses to check whether you earned enough wages.
- Standard base period: the first four of the last five completed calendar quarters before your claim begins.
- Alternate base period: if you do not qualify with the standard base period, EDD may use the last four completed calendar quarters.

Example: For a claim filed in January, February, or March, the standard base period is October 1 two years before through September 30 of the prior year.

## Waiting week
The first week of an eligible claim is an unpaid waiting period. You must still certify for it. You are not paid for the waiting week.

## Taxes
UI benefits are taxable income on your federal tax return but not on your California state income tax return. You can choose to have federal income tax withheld. EDD issues Form 1099G each January.
